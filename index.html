Box Cricket guide

How to Play

  1. Swipe forward inside the court to play a shot.
  2. Angle your swipe left or right to choose the shot direction.
  3. Turn Loft on for a higher, riskier shot.
  4. Strike when the marker is near the green Perfect zone.
  5. Hit the numbered walls to score 1, 2, 4, or 6.
  6. Fielders can stop ground shots or catch lofted shots.
  7. Chase 40 runs in 4 overs.
  8. After Lohit is out, Samrat may be unlocked through the optional reward choice.
