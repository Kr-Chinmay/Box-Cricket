# Box Cricket — Optional Gameplay Backlog

These are approved ideas for later discussion and are not part of the current implementation.

1. **Live tutorial** — an interactive guided first-over experience, in addition to the short How to Play screen.
2. **Wide balls and no-balls** — add realistic extra-delivery rules after the basic match flow is stable.
3. **Delivery-outcome balance** — limit how often a missed shot results in a bowled dismissal. Mix balls that hit the stumps with balls that pass them, so missed shots do not feel unfair or repetitive.
4. **Bowling variations** — introduce controlled off-spin and leg-spin deliveries after the standard underarm delivery is tuned.

When reporting Box Cricket gameplay-design progress, surface these as future options alongside the active roadmap.
